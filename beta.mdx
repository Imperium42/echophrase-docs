# Beta Program

Echophrase is currently in **open beta**. The free tier always offers 100% offline transcription, is unlimited, and account-free - signing in is only for Pro, and every signed-in user gets full Pro access at no cost while we polish the experience.

## What You Get

Echophrase transcribes fully offline (after a one-time model download on first run) with unlimited transcriptions and no account at all. During the beta period, anyone who signs in additionally receives **Pro tier** automatically. [Join the Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Er6NUcx) to share feedback and help shape the app.

<Note>
The Free tier transcribes 100% offline and never requires an account or internet connection to work. During the beta, signing in additionally upgrades you to Pro at no cost - it's entirely optional. The 5 second-5 minute recording length is a user-configurable setting (Settings -> Recording -> Max duration), identical on every tier - it is not a usage cap, and transcriptions themselves are unlimited on all tiers. Recording simply auto-stops and transcribes at that length so a single clip can't exhaust your GPU's memory.
</Note>

## What Happens After Beta

Once the beta period ends:

- **Pro becomes a paid subscription** (monthly or annual)
- **Free tier remains 100% offline for transcription and unlimited**, no account required, forever
- Your account and settings carry over seamlessly if you signed in for Pro
- Every account then starts a fresh 14-day Pro trial - the trial doesn't run during beta, since beta users already have Pro, so you get the full 14 days once beta ends

<Note>
All voice processing remains 100% local regardless of tier. Your audio never leaves your machine.
</Note>

## Beta Participant Perk

As a thank-you for helping shape Echophrase during beta, you can **lock in 50% off Pro for life**. Apply the coupon code **`BETA50`** at checkout on the [pricing page](https://echophrase.com/pricing).

This is a grandfathered rate, not a first-year promo: once you subscribe with `BETA50`, you keep the 50% discount for as long as your subscription stays active. It only ends if you cancel.

<Warning>
You must lock in the rate **before the beta ends**. Once we launch, the `BETA50` code stops working - so subscribe while the beta is still running to keep the discount for life.
</Warning>

## How to Join

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Echophrase">
    Grab the latest build from the [download page](/download). Start transcribing right after the one-time model download - no account, no sign-up, fully offline transcription from then on.

  </Step>
