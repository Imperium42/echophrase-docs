# Beta Program

Echophrase is currently in **open beta**. The free tier is always 100% offline, unlimited, and account-free - signing in is only for Pro, and every signed-in user gets full Pro access at no cost while we polish the experience.

## What You Get

Echophrase works fully offline with unlimited transcriptions and no account at all. During the beta period, anyone who signs in additionally receives **Pro tier** automatically. [Join the Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Er6NUcx) to share feedback and help shape the app.

<Note>
The Free tier is 100% offline and never requires an account or internet connection. During the beta, signing in additionally upgrades you to Pro at no cost - it's entirely optional.
</Note>

## What Happens After Beta

Once the beta period ends:

- **Pro becomes a paid subscription** (monthly or annual)
- **Free tier remains 100% offline and unlimited**, no account required, forever
- Your account and settings carry over seamlessly if you signed in for Pro

<Note>
All voice processing remains 100% local regardless of tier. Your audio never leaves your machine.
</Note>

## Beta Participant Perk

As a thank-you for helping shape Echophrase during beta, you can **lock in 50% off Pro for life**. Apply the coupon code **`BETA50`** at checkout on the [pricing page](https://echophrase.com/pricing).

This is a grandfathered rate, not a first-year promo: once you subscribe with `BETA50`, you keep the 50% discount for as long as your subscription stays active. It only ends if you cancel.

<Warning>
You must lock in the rate **before the beta ends**. Once we launch, the `BETA50` code stops working - so subscribe while the beta is still running to keep the discount for life.
</Warning>

## How to Join

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Echophrase">
    Grab the latest build from the [download page](/download). Start transcribing immediately - no account, no sign-up, fully offline.
