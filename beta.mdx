# Beta Program

Echophrase is currently in **open beta**. Every authenticated user gets full Pro access at no cost while we polish the experience.

## What You Get

During the beta period, all signed-in users receive **Pro tier** automatically:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Unlimited Transcriptions" icon="infinity">
    No daily or monthly caps on recording time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Large GPU Models" icon="bolt">
    Access to larger, more accurate Whisper and Parakeet models.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto-Paste" icon="clipboard">
    Transcriptions paste directly to your cursor, a file, or custom metadata.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Advanced Customization" icon="sliders">
    Fine-tune hotkeys, output formatting, and processing settings.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What Happens After Beta

Once the beta period ends:

- **Pro becomes a paid subscription** (monthly or annual)
- **Free tier is always available** with a 5-minute daily recording limit, basic models, and local storage
- Your account and settings carry over seamlessly

<Note>
All voice processing remains 100% local regardless of tier. Your audio never leaves your machine.
</Note>

## Beta Participant Perk

As a thank-you for helping shape Echophrase during beta, every beta participant will receive **50% off Pro for your first year** when paid plans launch.

No action required - your participation is tracked automatically. Just use the app while it's in beta and the discount is yours.
