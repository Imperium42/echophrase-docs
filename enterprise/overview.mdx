# Echophrase for Teams

Echophrase for Teams is the Enterprise tier: a per-seat plan for an **organization**. Every seat gets the full Pro feature set, signs in with a work identity, and is managed centrally by the organization's admins.

<Note>
**Early access.** Teams is rolling out to organizations by invitation. [Contact us](https://echophrase.com/contact) to set up your organization and single sign-on.
</Note>

## What does not change

Transcription is exactly as private on a Teams seat as on the Free tier. Audio is processed on the member's own GPU and never reaches our servers or the organization. The organization never sees transcript content, and neither do we.

The only per-seat signals the organization can see are the ones needed to run the plan: who holds a seat, their role, and sign-in and seat-change events in the audit log. If your organization turns on usage reporting, it also receives daily totals per member (minutes dictated and word count), and the app tells you so in **Settings > Organization**.

## What members see in the app

- **Sign in with SSO** on the login screen. Enter your work email and Echophrase takes you to your organization's sign-in page.
- **Settings > Organization** shows the organization name, your role (Member, Admin or Owner), a "Your account is managed by" notice and the seat count.
- **Settings > Data** shows a small lock on the anonymous app stats toggle when your organization or your IT department has decided it for you.
- Everything else is the regular Pro experience, including the 90-day offline grace period described in [Working Offline](/features/offline).

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|------|-----|
| Member | Use Echophrase with Pro features |
| Admin | Everything a member can, plus invite and remove members and change roles |
| Owner | Everything an admin can, plus seat and plan changes |
