# Welcome to Echophrase

Echophrase is a **privacy-first desktop application** that transforms your voice into text using state-of-the-art AI - all processed locally on your machine.

Echophrase is **free, open-source software** (MPL-2.0) with no account, no sign-in, and no tiers. Every feature is free for everyone, commercial use included. Install, grab a model (a one-time download on first run), and start dictating - transcription works 100% offline after that.

## How It Works

1. **Press your hotkey** to start recording
2. **Speak naturally** - Echophrase listens
3. **Press it again** and your words appear as text, typed wherever your cursor is

No copy-paste. No switching windows. Just speak and it types.
