## Your voice never leaves your computer

Transcription runs **entirely on your device**. Your microphone audio and the text it produces are processed by AI models running locally on your GPU - no audio and no transcript is ever uploaded, streamed, or sent to any server.

That guarantee is about your audio and text, not about the app being silent on the network. Echophrase also downloads models you pick, checks for updates, and, in official builds, sends anonymous app stats if you allow it (see below). None of those requests contain audio or transcripts - the full host list is [below](#what-the-app-talks-to).

## What the app talks to

Transcription never touches the network. These are the only hosts the desktop app contacts - check them against a packet capture if you like:

| Destination | When | Carries audio or text? |
|------|------|------|
| `huggingface.co` | Downloading a model you selected | No |
| `cdn.crabnebula.app` | Update check on launch | No |
| `telemetry.echophrase.com` | Anonymous app stats, official builds only and only if you allow it (see below) | No |

## What we do collect

We collect **anonymous, non-identifiable telemetrics** to make better product decisions - like which GPU backends people use, which AI models are most popular, and what the best default settings should be. None of this is tied to a user ID or account.

### Consent and opting out

Echophrase asks on first run, with the box pre-checked, and **every event respects your answer** - including the install count. You can change it anytime in **Settings > Data > "Send anonymous app stats"**: one toggle, no confirmation prompt. One final event is sent to confirm the opt-out, then all tracking stops. Launching with `DO_NOT_TRACK=1` (or `ECHOPHRASE_NO_TELEMETRY=1`) overrides the setting entirely. Disabling telemetrics does not affect app functionality in any way.

## No accounts

Echophrase has no accounts, sign-in, or payments, so we hold no personal profile of you. Builds compiled from source never send usage stats at all.

### GDPR & data rights

Because app stats carry no user or device identifier, we cannot link them to you. To stop sending them, turn off **Settings > Data > "Send anonymous app stats"**. See our full [Privacy Policy](https://echophrase.com/privacy) for your rights under GDPR and CCPA.
