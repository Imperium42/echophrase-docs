## Your voice never leaves your computer

Transcription runs **entirely on your device**. Your microphone audio and the text it produces are processed by AI models running locally on your GPU - no audio and no transcript is ever uploaded, streamed, or sent to any server.

That guarantee is about your audio and text, not about the app being silent on the network. Echophrase also downloads models you pick and, in official builds, checks for updates. None of those requests contain audio or transcripts - the full host list is [below](#what-the-app-talks-to).

<Card title="We never see your voice or text" icon="shield-halved">
  Audio stays in memory and is never written to disk unless you choose to save it. Transcription text stays in your clipboard or file - we have zero access to it. There is no "cloud processing" option. This is by design.
</Card>

## What the app talks to

Transcription never touches the network. These are the only hosts the desktop app contacts - check them against a packet capture if you like:

| Destination | When | Carries audio or text? |
|------|------|------|
| `huggingface.co` | Downloading a model you selected | No |
| `cdn.crabnebula.app` | Update check on launch | No |

## No telemetry, no analytics

The desktop app has **no telemetry and no analytics**: no usage stats, no crash reports, no install counts. The only network traffic is model downloads and update checks, listed above. Details are on [No Telemetry](/privacy/telemetry).

Our website uses cookieless [Umami](https://github.com/umami-software/umami) page-view analytics (page views, referrer, browser type, country), self-hosted, with no tracking across sites. It honors your browser's Global Privacy Control signal. This applies to the website only, never to the app.

Older versions of the app (before this change) could send anonymous usage stats if you allowed it. Updating removes that.

## No accounts

Echophrase has no accounts, sign-in, or payments, so we hold no personal profile of you. The app sends no usage stats.

### GDPR & data rights

The app collects no personal data, so there is nothing to link to you. See our full [Privacy Policy](https://echophrase.com/privacy) for your rights under GDPR and CCPA.
