# Quickstart

Get Echophrase running on your machine in just a few steps. No sign-up required - the Free tier is unlimited and 100% offline after a one-time model download on first run.

## 1. Download & Install

Download the latest version for your platform from the [downloads page](/download).

## 2. Launch Echophrase

Open Echophrase from your applications menu. On first launch, it will download the default AI model (Parakeet-TDT 0.6B v3, ~2.5 GB).

## 3. Grant Microphone Access

When prompted, allow Echophrase to access your microphone. Your audio is processed locally - nothing is sent to the cloud.

## 4. Start Transcribing

1. Click in any text field (browser, editor, chat app)
2. Press your recording hotkey - **ctrl+shift+space** (Windows/Linux) or **F5**
   (Mac - the Dictation key, mic icon)
3. Speak naturally
4. Press the hotkey again to stop - your words appear as text

That's it! You're ready to go.
