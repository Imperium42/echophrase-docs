# Input Modes

Echophrase offers different modes to match your workflow.

## Push-to-Talk (Default)

Press your hotkey to start recording, then press it again to stop and transcribe.

**Best for**: Quick voice inputs, commands, short messages

## Open Mic

Echophrase listens continuously and transcribes each utterance automatically
after a configurable pause in speech (the **silence timeout**, 2-10 seconds).

**Best for**: Longer dictation, hands-free operation

## Switching Modes

1. Open Echophrase settings
2. Navigate to **Audio Input**
3. Select **Push-to-Talk** or **Open Mic**
