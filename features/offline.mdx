# Working Offline

Echophrase is built to work offline - including in airplane mode. The **Free tier offers 100% offline transcription and is unlimited** - no account and no internet connection required, ever. Transcription itself never needs the internet - audio is processed locally on your GPU, and your voice never leaves your device.

<Note>
**One exception: the first run.** Models are downloaded on demand, so you need an
internet connection once - to fetch whichever model(s) you want to use. After
that model is on disk, transcription never touches the network again. The default
Parakeet-TDT 0.6B v3 is ~2.5 GB; see [Models](/config/models) for the others.
</Note>

<Note>
**What about telemetry?** Separately from transcription, Echophrase collects anonymous, non-identifiable usage telemetry by default - things like GPU backend, selected model, and settings/session events, plus crash reports - never audio or transcript content. The consent box is pre-checked on first run, but every event honors your answer, and you can opt out anytime in Settings > Data > "Send anonymous app stats" (or launch with `DO_NOT_TRACK=1`). See the full event-by-event list at [What We Collect](/privacy/telemetry). With no internet connection (airplane mode included), there's nothing to send either way - transcription works exactly the same.
</Note>

Transcription never contacts our servers, so there is no online requirement to fall out of compliance with. If you never sign in, this entire page could be one sentence: **it just works, offline, forever.**

## Pro Subscriptions & Offline (Pro Only)

If you've signed in for **Pro**, the app refreshes your entitlements online roughly once a day, silently in the background. If your device stays fully offline, Pro features keep working for a **90-day offline grace period**. Past that, the app simply runs as the (still unlimited) Free tier until you reconnect - nothing is deleted, your account stays active, and your subscription is not cancelled. Why 90 days? It's long enough for travel, outages, and air-gapped setups, while keeping entitlement state honest - and it applies only to Pro.

You can sign in with **email (passwordless magic link)** or **Google** - no password to remember or leak. Once signed in, your session's refresh token is valid for **30 days**: as long as you reconnect within that window, you're kept signed in silently - no re-entering a magic link or re-authenticating with Google every session. Reconnect after more than 30 days offline and you'll need to sign in again (your 90-day Pro grace period is a separate clock - the two don't have to line up).

<Tip>
  Reconnecting and signing in restores your paid entitlements **immediately** - no waiting, no re-purchase, no support ticket.
</Tip>

## What You Won't Lose

**On the Free tier: nothing - there is no account or server dependency to lose.** For Pro subscribers past the 90-day grace period:

<Warning>
  Going past the 90-day Pro grace period **never** deletes your data, locks you out, or wipes your settings.
</Warning>

- Your transcriptions and any local content remain intact
- Your account stays active
- Your subscription is not cancelled
- Your settings, hotkeys, and downloaded models are untouched

The only thing that changes is which Pro-only features are available, and that reverses the moment you sign in again.

## Cloud-Only Features

A few features intentionally require a connection because they sync with our servers:

- Cloud sync of application settings (Pro, opt-in - settings only, never audio or transcriptions)
- Account management and billing
- Subscription tier upgrades / downgrades

These will simply be unavailable when offline. They do not affect transcription itself.
