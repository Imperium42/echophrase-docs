# Working Offline

Echophrase is built to work offline. The **Free tier is 100% offline and unlimited** - no account, no internet connection, no server round-trip, ever. Transcription itself never needs the internet - audio is processed locally on your GPU, and your voice never leaves your device.

## Periodic Re-Authentication (Pro Only)

This section applies **only if you've signed in for Pro**. The Free tier never talks to our servers and has no re-authentication requirement at all.

To verify your **Pro subscription**, your device must reconnect to our authentication server **at least once every 90 days**. We call this the **Pro offline grace period**.

<Note>
  Reconnecting takes a fraction of a second. You don't need to do anything manually - the app does it in the background whenever it detects an internet connection.
</Note>

### What Happens At The 90-Day Mark (Pro Only)

| Tier | Online | Offline ≤ 90 days | Offline > 90 days |
|------|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Free** (no account) | Unlimited, 100% offline | Unlimited, 100% offline | Unlimited, 100% offline - nothing changes, ever |
| **Pro / Enterprise** | Unlimited + Pro features | Unlimited + Pro features | Temporarily downgraded to the Free tier feature set (still unlimited, but no Pro-only features) |

<Tip>
  Reconnecting and signing in restores your paid entitlements **immediately** - no waiting, no re-purchase, no support ticket.
</Tip>

## What You Won't Lose

<Warning>
  Going past the 90-day Pro grace period **never** deletes your data, locks you out, or wipes your settings.
</Warning>

- Your transcriptions and any local content remain intact
- Your account stays active
- Your subscription is not cancelled
- Your settings, hotkeys, and downloaded models are untouched

The only thing that changes is which Pro-only feature limits apply, and that change reverses the moment you sign in again. Free-tier users are never affected, since the Free tier has no account and no grace period to begin with.

## Why The 90-Day Window?

We don't want to ping our servers constantly - that would defeat the privacy-first design. But we also need a way to verify a Pro subscriber's entitlement is still active. Ninety days is long enough to handle most travel, network outages, and air-gapped scenarios without inconvenience, while short enough to keep entitlement state honest. This window only applies to Pro; the Free tier is unaffected because it's offline by design.

## Cloud-Only Features

A few features intentionally require a connection because they sync with our servers:

- Cloud sync of transcription history (Pro / Enterprise, opt-in)
- Account management and billing
- Subscription tier upgrades / downgrades

These will simply be unavailable when offline. They do not affect transcription itself.
